# Private Beta Consent Notice

Current status: draft participant-facing notice. Requires legal/privacy owner review before use. This draft does not claim legal, privacy, medical, security, or regulatory compliance.

## Plain-Language Summary

You are being invited to join a limited private beta for BecomingOS.

BecomingOS is pre-MVP, experimental, educational, and non-clinical. It is designed to support reflection and action planning. It is not an emergency service, therapy, diagnosis, treatment, crisis intervention, legal advice, medical advice, financial advice, addiction treatment advice, or a replacement for professional help.

Participation is optional. You can choose what to provide, decline optional data categories, withdraw from the beta, and request deletion of your beta data.

## What You Are Testing

The private beta may test reflection, planning, AI-drafted suggestions, memory confirmation, Today Directive, Bad Day Mode, and optional support/accountability concepts.

AI outputs are drafts. You should review, edit, reject, or confirm them. Nothing becomes durable memory without your confirmation.

## Data Categories

You may be asked to provide some or all of the following data categories. Each category should be optional unless a specific beta task cannot work without it.

| Data category | Purpose | Optionality |
|---|---|---|
| Manual entries | Reflection, planning, memory candidates, and beta feedback. | Optional except for tasks you choose to complete. |
| Calendar | Planning context, time windows, and Today Directive timing. | Optional. |
| Health/wearable signals | Optional context for energy, activity, or recovery-aware planning. | Optional and sensitive. |
| Tasks | Planning context and action tracking. | Optional. |
| Documents | Optional context for reflection or planning tasks. | Optional and sensitive. |
| Photos | Optional context if a beta task asks for visual input. | Optional and sensitive. |
| Audio | Optional context, transcription, or reflection capture. | Optional and sensitive. |
| Video | Optional context if a beta task asks for video input. | Optional and sensitive. |
| Support circle contacts | Optional support/accountability permissions. | Optional and sensitive. |

## AI Provider Disclosure

Some beta features may use AI providers to draft summaries, reflections, recommendations, or memory candidates.

No live beta user data may be sent to an AI provider until provider retention, logging, training-use, deletion, subprocessors, transfer terms, security, and contractual terms are reviewed and approved.

If AI provider processing is used, you must be told what categories of data may be processed and for what purpose.

## Retention

The proposed default beta retention period is 90 days unless the owner approves a different period before beta starts.

Beta data must be deleted or anonymised after the beta unless you give renewed consent under a future approved policy.

## Deletion, Export/Access, And Withdrawal

You may request:

- Deletion of beta data.
- Access/export where applicable.
- Withdrawal from beta.
- Withdrawal of consent for future processing.

Deleted, rejected, unconfirmed, expired, archived, or consent-withdrawn data must not be used for future AI retrieval, recommendations, Today plans, Ask AI, analytics, embeddings, or future outputs.

## No Production Reuse

Your beta data will not be reused in production without renewed consent.

## Safety Boundary

BecomingOS is not emergency monitoring or crisis response. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or an appropriate crisis support service.

Support Circle features, if tested, do not automatically alert people unless you have given prior consent and clear rules exist.

## Consent Statement

By joining the private beta, you confirm that:

- You understand BecomingOS is pre-MVP, experimental, educational, and non-clinical.
- You understand participation is optional.
- You understand what data categories may be collected.
- You understand AI providers may process data only after provider review and disclosure.
- You understand beta data retention, deletion, withdrawal, and no-production-reuse rules.
- You understand BecomingOS is not an emergency service or professional advice provider.
