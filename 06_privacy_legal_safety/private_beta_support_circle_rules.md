# Private Beta Support Circle Rules

Current status: approval-ready draft. Requires product, safety, privacy/legal, and owner approval before any Support Circle beta use. This document does not approve private beta, implementation, public MVP build, production readiness, or compliance claims.

## Boundary

Support Circle must not be used as crisis response, emergency monitoring, therapy, diagnosis, treatment, or professional advice.

BecomingOS does not monitor users in real time and does not provide live rescue.

## Core Rules

- Support Circle is opt-in only.
- No support contact may be added without user action.
- No automatic alerting may occur without explicit prior user consent and clear rules.
- Sharing must be purpose-limited, time-limited, and revocable.
- The user must choose what can be shared, with whom, for how long, and why.
- Support Circle contacts must not receive crisis or sensitive content unless separately approved by explicit user consent and reviewed by safety/privacy/legal owners.

## What Can Be Shared

Permitted sharing options may include only approved, minimal categories, such as:

- Check-in status category.
- Completion status category.
- User-written support request.
- Permission scope.
- Expiry date.

Exact sharing options must be approved before beta use.

## What Cannot Be Shared

Do not share by default:

- Manual reflection text.
- AI draft text.
- Documents, photos, audio, or video.
- Calendar details.
- Health/wearable details.
- Crisis text.
- Safety logs.
- Diagnosis labels.
- Private memory content.
- Support contact data with other contacts.

## Support Contact Consent

If support contacts participate, they must understand:

- BecomingOS is not emergency monitoring.
- They are not being enrolled as crisis responders.
- Shared information is limited to the user's approved scope.
- Sharing may expire or be revoked.

## Expiry And Revocation

Each sharing grant should have:

- Scope.
- Recipient.
- Purpose.
- Start date.
- Expiry date or review date.
- Revocation process.

Revoked sharing must stop future sharing and remove active permissions.

## Audit Trail Concept

Maintain a minimal audit trail for:

- Permission granted.
- Permission changed.
- Permission revoked.
- Expiry reached.
- Support contact notice/consent status, if applicable.

Do not include sensitive free text in the audit trail.

## Crisis Boundary

If crisis or immediate danger appears, BecomingOS should signpost appropriate emergency or human support. Support Circle must not be represented as crisis response, emergency monitoring, or guaranteed help.
