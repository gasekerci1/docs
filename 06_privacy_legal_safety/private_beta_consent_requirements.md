# Private Beta Consent Requirements

Current status: consent requirements for limited private beta planning. Legal review is required before broad release.

## Consent Notice Requirements

Private beta testers must receive a clear consent notice before providing real data.

The notice must explain:

- BecomingOS is pre-MVP and experimental.
- Participation is optional.
- The product is educational and non-clinical.
- The product is not therapy, diagnosis, treatment, crisis intervention, emergency monitoring, legal advice, medical advice, financial advice, or professional advice.
- What data may be collected.
- Why each data category is collected.
- Which data categories are optional.
- How long data is retained.
- How participants can edit, delete, access, export, or withdraw their data.
- Whether AI providers process data.
- Whether AI provider retention, training, logging, and deletion terms apply.
- That beta data will be deleted or anonymised after beta unless renewed consent is obtained.
- That beta data will not be reused in production without renewed consent.

## Data Categories

Consent must cover each applicable category separately:

- Manual entries.
- Calendar.
- Health/wearable signals.
- Tasks.
- Documents.
- Photos.
- Audio.
- Video.
- Support circle contacts.

## Purpose And Optionality

Each data category must have a stated purpose. Users must be able to decline optional categories without losing access to unrelated beta features where practical.

## Retention

The beta must define a retention period before data collection begins. A 90-day retention limit is suggested unless the owner approves a shorter or legally required alternative.

## Deletion, Access, Export, And Withdrawal

Participants must be able to request deletion, access/export where applicable, and withdrawal from beta.

Deleted, rejected, withdrawn, or unconfirmed data must not be used by AI memory, retrieval, recommendations, analytics, or future production systems.

## Legal Review

Legal review is required before broad release, public beta, commercial launch, production reuse of beta data, or any compliance claim.
