# Legal Claims Policy

Current status: pre-MVP claims policy. This document does not provide legal advice, claim legal compliance, approve public MVP build, approve production readiness, or approve public launch.

Legal review is required before broad release, compliance claims, app-store copy, marketing copy, paid launch, or public MVP/build claims.

## Allowed Claims

Allowed claims must be conservative, accurate, and traceable to approved source-of-truth decisions.

Examples:

- "BecomingOS is a pre-MVP educational reflection and action-planning system."
- "BecomingOS is not therapy, diagnosis, treatment, crisis support, or professional advice."
- "AI outputs are drafts for user review."
- "Nothing becomes durable memory without user confirmation."
- "A limited private beta may be planned for trusted participants with explicit consent."
- "The current specification is conceptual."
- "No public build or production readiness is approved."

## Prohibited Claims

Do not claim or imply that BecomingOS provides:

- Therapy.
- Diagnosis.
- Treatment.
- Crisis intervention.
- Emergency monitoring.
- Legal advice.
- Medical advice.
- Financial advice.
- Addiction treatment advice.
- Professional advice or professional care replacement.
- Guaranteed outcomes.
- Guaranteed behaviour change.
- Guaranteed wellbeing improvement.
- Crisis detection or rescue.
- Real-time monitoring.
- Production readiness.
- Public MVP/build availability.
- Legal compliance.
- Validated market demand.
- Validated pricing or willingness-to-pay.
- Clinically tested results.

## Public MVP And Production Language

Do not use public MVP, build-ready, launch-ready, production-ready, enterprise-ready, clinically safe, compliant, or similar claims unless future owner-approved evidence gates and legal review explicitly allow them.

Readiness reports are evidence only. They are not approvals.

## AI Claims

AI may be described as drafting reflections, options, summaries, or recommendations for user review.

Do not describe AI as a therapist, clinician, counsellor, coach, emergency responder, professional adviser, autonomous decision-maker, or source of truth.

AI recommendations must explain or cite which confirmed user context influenced them when such recommendations are provided.

## Safe Wording Examples

- "This is a draft reflection. Review, edit, or reject it before saving."
- "This suggestion is based on confirmed context you chose to save."
- "You can delete or edit saved information."
- "If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. BecomingOS is not an emergency service."
- "This private beta is experimental and limited to trusted participants."
- "You can withdraw from the beta and request deletion of your beta data."

## Unsafe Wording Examples

- "BecomingOS diagnoses your patterns."
- "Your AI therapist."
- "Crisis monitoring built in."
- "Production-ready MVP."
- "Clinically proven transformation."
- "Guaranteed clarity in 30 days."
- "We comply with all privacy laws."
- "The app automatically alerts your support circle when risk is detected."

## Review Requirement

Any public-facing copy, beta consent copy, support copy, app-store copy, marketing material, or compliance statement requires legal/safety review before broad release.
