# Safety Boundaries

## Product Boundary

BecomingOS is an educational, non-clinical reflection and action-planning system.

It is not an emergency service and does not monitor users in real time.

## Prohibited Positioning

BecomingOS does not provide:

- Therapy.
- Diagnosis.
- Treatment.
- Crisis intervention.
- Legal advice.
- Medical advice.
- Financial advice.
- Addiction treatment advice.
- Emergency response.
- Professional advice or professional care replacement.

## Signposting

Where relevant, BecomingOS may signpost users to appropriate human support, emergency support, or professional services.

Signposting must not imply monitoring, rescue, treatment, clinical assessment, or guaranteed support.

## Support Circle

Support Circle and accountability features are optional and user-controlled.

The product cannot automatically alert people unless prior user consent, clear rules, and documented boundaries exist. Even with consent, Support Circle must not be represented as emergency monitoring or crisis response.

## AI Boundary

AI may draft reflections, options, plans, and recommendations for user review.

AI must not act as a therapist, clinician, emergency responder, professional adviser, or decision-maker.
