# Private Beta Final Consent Notice

Current status: approved for limited private beta use as of 2026-06-01. This notice does not approve public MVP build, production readiness, or any legal/privacy/security/regulatory compliance claim.

## Invitation

You are invited to take part in a limited, non-public private beta for BecomingOS.

BecomingOS is pre-MVP and experimental. It is an educational, non-clinical reflection and action-planning system. It is designed to help with reflection, planning, AI-drafted suggestions, and user-confirmed memory.

BecomingOS is not therapy, diagnosis, treatment, crisis support, emergency monitoring, legal advice, medical advice, financial advice, addiction treatment advice, or a replacement for professional help.

Participation is optional. You can choose what to provide, skip optional data categories, and withdraw from the beta.

## What You May Test

The private beta may include:

- Reflection and planning tasks.
- AI-drafted summaries, recommendations, or memory candidates.
- Memory confirmation flows.
- Today Directive planning.
- Bad Day Mode.
- Life Capture inputs.
- Optional Support Circle permissions.

AI outputs are drafts. Review, edit, reject, or confirm them before relying on them. Nothing becomes durable memory without your confirmation.

## Retention

Beta data will be retained for up to 90 days unless the owner approves a shorter period or a legally required alternative before beta data collection begins. The 90-day period begins when the beta data is collected and ends 90 days after that collection date, unless the data is deleted earlier at your request or anonymised/deleted after the beta.

After the beta, beta data will be deleted or anonymised unless you give renewed consent under a future approved policy.

## Deletion, Access, Export, And Withdrawal

To request deletion, access, export, correction, or withdrawal, email gasekerci@gmail.com or use the in-app support form.

You may request:

- Access to beta data where applicable.
- Export of beta data where applicable.
- Correction of beta data.
- Deletion of beta data.
- Withdrawal from the beta.
- Withdrawal of consent for future processing.

Deleted, rejected, unconfirmed, expired, archived, or consent-withdrawn data must not be used for future AI retrieval, recommendations, Today plans, Ask AI, analytics, embeddings, or future outputs.

## No Production Reuse

Your beta data will not be reused in production without renewed consent.

## Crisis And Emergency Boundary

BecomingOS is not an emergency service and does not monitor you in real time. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or appropriate crisis support.

Support Circle features, if tested, are not crisis response and do not automatically alert anyone unless you have given prior explicit consent and clear rules exist.
