# OpenAI Services Agreement — Markdown Evidence Extract

Source URL: https://openai.com/policies/services-agreement/  
Retrieved via ChatGPT web tool: 2026-06-01  
Role in evidence pack: Primary API/business customer agreement.

## Key extracted evidence

- Updated: December 1, 2025. Effective: January 1, 2026.
- The agreement applies to OpenAI APIs, ChatGPT Enterprise, ChatGPT Business, ChatGPT for Clinicians, and other business/developer services, and does not apply to consumer/individual services unless specified.
- The agreement is between the customer organization and the applicable OpenAI contracting party.
- Customer may use OpenAI’s API to integrate services into customer applications and make customer applications available to end users.
- Customer must obtain and maintain from end users any consents necessary to allow OpenAI to deliver the services.
- As between customer and OpenAI, customer retains ownership rights in input and owns output to the extent permitted by law.
- OpenAI will only use customer content as necessary to provide services, comply with law, enforce OpenAI policies, and prevent abuse.
- OpenAI will not use customer content to develop or improve services unless customer explicitly agrees.
- Customer is responsible for input and warrants that it has all rights, licences and permissions required to provide input to the services.
- OpenAI has completed independent audits evaluating security policies, procedures and controls, and may provide the most recent audit reports to customer on written request, no more than once per year.
- If customer uses services to process personal data, OpenAI and customer must comply with the DPA incorporated into the agreement.
- On termination, OpenAI will delete customer content from its systems within 30 days unless legally required to retain it or otherwise agreed in writing.
- Beta services may not be supported, may change without notice, may not be as reliable or available, and may not have been subjected to the same security measures and auditing as the services.
