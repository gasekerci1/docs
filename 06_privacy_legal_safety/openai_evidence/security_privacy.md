## Key extracted evidence

- OpenAI states that it does not train models on organization data by default for business users.
- OpenAI states that enhanced data retention controls help customers stay compliant.
- OpenAI states customer content is encrypted at rest and in transit between customer and OpenAI, and between OpenAI and service providers.
- OpenAI states it offers a Data Processing Addendum and Business Associate Agreement for customers.
- OpenAI states infrastructure supporting API and ChatGPT business products has been evaluated by an independent third-party auditor for security and confidentiality controls.
- OpenAI states its API and ChatGPT business product services have undergone independent SOC 2 Type 2 examination for Security, Availability, Confidentiality and Privacy.
- OpenAI states it maintains ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and ISO/IEC 27701:2019 certifications for information security and privacy management systems supporting OpenAI API, ChatGPT Enterprise and ChatGPT Edu.
- OpenAI states it maintains ISO/IEC 42001:2023 AI Management System covering consumer and business AI products and models in its role as AI producer/provider.
- OpenAI states infrastructure runs on trusted cloud providers using industry best practices including encryption in transit and at rest, change management and strict access controls.
- The page links to the Trust Portal and security whitepaper.
- OpenAI business products support administrative features such as audit logs, data residency for API, account security and fine-grained controls.
