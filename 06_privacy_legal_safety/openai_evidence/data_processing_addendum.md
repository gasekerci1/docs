# OpenAI Data Processing Addendum — Markdown Evidence Extract

Source URL: https://openai.com/policies/data-processing-addendum/  
Retrieved via ChatGPT web tool: 2026-06-01  
Role in evidence pack: Primary processor, breach, subprocessor, transfer and customer-obligation evidence.

## Key extracted evidence

- As part of providing services under the agreement, OpenAI may process customer data on behalf of the customer.
- OpenAI acts as a Data Processor on the customer’s behalf.
- OpenAI processes customer data only for the purpose of delivering services under the agreement and DPA.
- OpenAI will notify the customer without undue delay after becoming aware of any Personal Data Breach.
- OpenAI will provide reasonable assistance to customer regarding personal data breaches.
- Customer gives general authorization for OpenAI to engage subprocessors listed in the Sub-Processor List.
- OpenAI will notify customer of changes to the Sub-Processor List by blog post, in-service notification, other reasonable means, or email if subscribed.
- Customer may object to a new subprocessor within 30 days of notice.
- OpenAI must enter into contractual arrangements with subprocessors imposing obligations comparable to those in the DPA, and OpenAI remains liable for subprocessor acts/omissions as stated in the DPA.
- Following expiry or termination, OpenAI will at customer instruction return or delete customer data and existing copies unless retention is required by law.
- Customer must provide necessary notices and maintain necessary rights, consents and authorizations to provide customer data to OpenAI and authorize processing.
- The DPA defines SCCs as the EU Commission standard contractual clauses for transfers to third countries.
- The DPA identifies the Sub-Processor List as available at https://platform.openai.com/subprocessors.
- The DPA references the UK Addendum to the EU SCCs.
- Schedule 1 says the nature and purpose of processing is performance of the services under the agreement.
- Customer may submit personal data to the services, categories depend on customer use, and unstructured data may include names, contact information, demographic information or other information provided by end users.
- Schedule notes no sensitive data is intended to be transferred unless the user includes it unexpectedly in unstructured data.
- Transfers to subprocessors are as per Article 2.9 and are for the duration of the agreement unless otherwise agreed.
