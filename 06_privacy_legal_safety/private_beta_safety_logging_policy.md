# Private Beta Safety Logging Policy

Current status: draft policy requiring owner, safety, privacy, legal, and security review. This document does not approve implementation, private beta start, public MVP build, production readiness, or compliance claims.

## Boundary

BecomingOS is not emergency monitoring, crisis response, therapy, diagnosis, treatment, or professional advice.

The product may signpost to appropriate human or emergency support where relevant, but it must not imply live rescue, monitoring, or clinical care.

## Safety Log Purpose

Safety logs, if used, may exist only to support narrow beta safety review and operational learning.

They must not be used as product memory, AI memory, recommendations context, analytics content, marketing evidence, or training data.

## Minimal Segregated Logs

Safety logs must be:

- Minimal.
- Segregated from product memory.
- Segregated from analytics.
- Access-controlled.
- Retention-limited.
- Reviewed by named owners.
- Approved by safety, privacy, and legal owners before beta use.

## Content Limits

Crisis/safety content must not become product memory.

Crisis text and sensitive free text must not be stored in analytics.

If a safety event must be recorded, prefer minimal classifications such as:

- Event category.
- Resource shown category.
- Timestamp or date band.
- Whether the user self-initiated help-seeking.
- Follow-up owner if manual review is approved.

Do not store full crisis messages, private reflections, diagnosis labels, self-harm details, or conversation transcripts unless separately approved by legal/privacy/safety owners.

## Access Control

Access must be limited to named people with an approved beta safety role.

Access should be reviewed before beta starts and after beta ends.

## Retention

Safety log retention must be defined before beta starts. It may differ from product data retention only if owner, privacy, legal, and safety owners approve it.

## Review Requirements

Before beta starts:

- Safety owner review required.
- Privacy/legal review required.
- Security review required.
- Owner approval required.
- Go/no-go decision must record whether safety logging is enabled, disabled, or deferred.
