# Production Privacy, Legal, And Safety Gate

Current status: production privacy/legal/safety gate only.

Default status: not approved.

This document does not approve private beta, public MVP build, production readiness, public launch, implementation, legal compliance, privacy compliance, medical compliance, security compliance, regulatory compliance, or safety compliance.

## Required Gates Before Production

| Gate | Required evidence | Current status |
|---|---|---|
| Final consent | Approved production consent and user-facing notices for data categories, optionality, AI provider processing, retention, deletion, export/access, and withdrawal. | Missing / not approved |
| Privacy policy | Approved production privacy policy or equivalent user-facing privacy notice. | Missing / not approved |
| Data inventory | Production data inventory covering manual entries, calendar, health/wearable signals, tasks, documents, photos, audio, video, support contacts, memories, embeddings, logs, analytics, and provider-held data. | Missing |
| Retention/deletion/export | Operational evidence for retention, deletion, anonymisation, export/access, correction, withdrawal, provider deletion, and embedding invalidation. | Missing |
| AI provider disclosure | Approved disclosure of AI providers, purpose, data categories, retention/logging/training/deletion terms, subprocessors, and transfers. | Missing / not approved |
| Crisis signposting | Approved crisis/emergency signposting policy that preserves the non-emergency-service boundary. | Missing |
| Legal claims | Approved public claims, launch copy, app-store copy, support copy, marketing copy, and prohibited-claims review. | Missing / not approved |
| Support boundaries | Approved support wording that excludes therapy, diagnosis, treatment, crisis intervention, emergency monitoring, legal advice, medical advice, financial advice, addiction treatment advice, and professional replacement. | Missing / not approved |
| Safety logging | Approved minimal, segregated, access-controlled, retention-limited safety logging policy, if safety logs are used. | Missing / not approved |
| Analytics privacy | Approved analytics taxonomy, consent, retention, deletion, and sensitive-content exclusions. | Missing / not approved |

## Non-Negotiable Boundaries

- No therapy.
- No diagnosis.
- No treatment.
- No crisis intervention.
- No emergency monitoring.
- No legal, medical, financial, addiction treatment, or professional advice.
- No automatic Support Circle alerts without explicit prior user consent and approved rules.
- No use of rejected, deleted, unconfirmed, expired, archived, or consent-withdrawn data.
- No AI provider use without approval.
- No compliance claim without review.
