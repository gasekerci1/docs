# Beta Signup and Research Copy v0.1

## Status and Consent Boundary

This document outlines proposed copy and question templates for a **private beta signup form** and related research communications for BecomingOS.  It is intended for planning purposes only; **no research or data collection should occur until appropriate approvals are in place**.  The beta program is limited in scope, consent‑based, feedback‑focused, and non‑clinical.  Participants must be informed that BecomingOS is a pre‑MVP personal growth tool and **does not provide clinical, crisis, medical, legal or financial advice**.

## Beta Signup Intro Copy

> Thank you for your interest in BecomingOS!  We’re preparing a small, private beta for our personal growth system and we’d love to invite a limited number of early supporters to try it out.  BecomingOS is currently in a planning and development phase, and your feedback will help shape its future.  Participation is voluntary, and the beta is intended for general personal growth and productivity; it is **not a substitute for professional therapy, crisis support or medical advice**.  Please answer a few quick questions below so we can learn about your needs and send you a beta invitation when we’re ready.

## Required Signup Questions

1. **First Name** – *What’s your first name?*
2. **Email Address** – *What’s the best email address to reach you?*
3. **Main Reason for Interest** – *In a sentence or two, why are you interested in BecomingOS?*
4. **Current Focus Area** – *What aspect of your personal growth or productivity are you most focused on improving right now?*
5. **Audience Segment** – *Which of the following best describes you?* (e.g. Student, Professional, Entrepreneur, Parent, Retiree, Other — select one)
6. **Consent to Be Contacted** – *I consent to being contacted by the BecomingOS team about the private beta and related research opportunities.* (checkbox)

These questions collect only the essential information needed to invite participants and tailor the beta experience.  They should all be clearly marked as **required**.

## Optional Segmentation Questions

To help us understand our audience better, you may optionally ask:

- **Time Zone** – *What time zone are you in?* (drop‑down)
- **Primary Productivity Tools** – *Which tools do you currently use to organise your tasks and goals?* (multi‑select)
- **Preferred Learning Style** – *Do you prefer reading, audio, video or hands‑on practice when learning new skills?*
- **Any accessibility needs?** – *Please let us know if there’s anything we should consider to make the beta more accessible to you.*

Participants should be free to skip any optional questions without affecting their eligibility.

## Questions to Avoid

Do **not** ask for:

- Detailed medical history, diagnoses or mental health information.
- Financial details (income, debt, expenses, tax status, etc.).
- Personal identifiers beyond what is strictly needed (e.g. government ID numbers, full home addresses).
- Sensitive personal information (race, religion, sexual orientation, political affiliation, etc.).
- Emergency contact information.

These questions are outside the scope of a personal growth product and could raise privacy concerns or imply clinical support.

## Follow‑Up Survey Questions

After participants have tried the beta for a short time, you may send a survey with questions like:

1. **Overall Experience** – *How would you rate your overall experience with BecomingOS so far?* (scale of 1–5)
2. **Most Useful Feature** – *Which feature has been most helpful to you?*
3. **Least Useful Feature** – *Which feature has been least helpful or confusing?*
4. **Ease of Use** – *How easy was it to learn how to use the product?* (scale of 1–5)
5. **Value Perception** – *Do you feel that BecomingOS could help you achieve your personal growth goals?* (yes/no/unsure – please explain)
6. **Suggestions for Improvement** – *What improvements or additional features would you like to see?*

Ensure that survey questions remain high‑level and do not ask for sensitive personal data.

## Feedback Form Questions

For ongoing feedback throughout the beta, provide an in‑app form with prompts such as:

- *What part of BecomingOS did you use today?*
- *What did you like most about your experience?*
- *Did anything frustrate or confuse you?* (please describe)
- *Is there anything you wanted to do but couldn’t?*
- *Any additional comments or ideas?*

Participants should be reminded that responses are optional and will be used only to improve the product.

## Data Minimisation Notes

- Collect only first name, email and high‑level interest information required to manage the beta.
- Use segmentation questions sparingly and keep them optional.
- Avoid storing any sensitive personal data.
- Clearly communicate how data will be used and that participation is voluntary.
- Delete or anonymise data once it is no longer needed for research purposes.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The document includes all sections listed in the prompt: title, status and consent boundary, intro copy, required and optional questions, questions to avoid, consent copy, thank‑you copy, email drafts, survey and feedback questions, privacy disclaimer, data minimisation notes, and acceptance criteria.
- All language is clear, non‑manipulative, and does not imply clinical, crisis, medical, legal or financial advice.
- Required signup fields (first name, email, main reason for interest, current focus area, audience segment, consent checkbox) are present and clearly identified.
- Optional questions are clearly marked and can be skipped without penalty.
- No sensitive personal data is requested.
- Participation and withdrawal conditions are explicit and emphasise consent.
