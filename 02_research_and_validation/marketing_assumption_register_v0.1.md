# Marketing Assumption Register v0.1

## Status and Use

This register documents the marketing assumptions that guide planning for BecomingOS during its pre‑MVP phase.  It is a governance tool intended to record assumptions, evidence, risks and validation plans.  Do **not** treat any assumption as a validated claim until supporting evidence has been gathered and recorded in this register.

## Assumption Categories

- **Target Audience** – who we believe will adopt the product.
- **Positioning** – the place BecomingOS occupies in the minds of its audience relative to alternatives.
- **Willingness to Pay** – assumptions about pricing and the value customers will attribute to the product.
- **Landing Page Conversion** – expected conversion rates and behaviours on our marketing site.
- **Privacy Trust** – assumptions about users’ trust in our handling of their personal data.
- **AI Memory Trust** – how comfortable users will be with the product’s AI remembering their context.
- **Private Beta Demand** – the level of interest in joining a private beta program.
- **Product Comprehension** – how well users understand the product’s purpose and benefits.
- **Feature Desirability** – which features resonate most with our audience.
- **Social Content Resonance** – which messages and content perform best across social channels.

## Evidence Level Definitions

1. **None** – no supporting data; the assumption is based on internal beliefs or hypotheses only.
2. **Anecdotal** – evidence from informal conversations or limited user feedback without systematic collection.
3. **Indirect Data** – evidence drawn from secondary research or comparable markets but not directly from our target audience.
4. **Empirical** – quantitative or qualitative data collected directly from our target audience (e.g. surveys, experiments).
5. **Validated** – sufficient empirical evidence has been gathered to substantiate the assumption, and no conflicting evidence exists.

## Validation Method Options

- **User Interviews** – conduct one‑on‑one discussions with target users to understand needs and perceptions.
- **Surveys** – distribute structured questionnaires to collect quantitative data from a larger audience.
- **A/B Tests** – compare variations of messaging or features on the landing page or within the product.
- **Analytics** – analyse behaviour and conversion metrics from the website or product usage.
- **Competitor Research** – study similar products and their market positioning, pricing and messaging.
- **Beta Test Results** – collect usage data and feedback during the private beta.

## Priority Ranking

Use a numeric scale of **1** (highest urgency) to **5** (lowest urgency) to prioritise assumptions for validation.  Assumptions with greater potential impact and uncertainty should be addressed first.

## Claims Not Allowed Until Validated

Marketing copy, sales materials, investor communications and public statements **must not** make claims based on these assumptions until they reach an **Empirical** or **Validated** evidence level.  Unvalidated assumptions should be described as hypotheses or potential benefits, not guarantees.

## Review Cadence

- **Monthly Reviews** – The marketing team should review this register monthly to update evidence levels, assess risks and adjust priorities.
- **After Key Milestones** – Conduct an additional review after major research activities (e.g. completion of a survey or beta phase).

## Acceptance Criteria

- Each assumption is recorded with an ID, category, evidence level, risk, validation method, priority, status and notes.
- Evidence levels and statuses are updated as data is collected.
- No marketing claims are made based on assumptions until they reach **Empirical** or **Validated** evidence levels.
- The register is reviewed regularly according to the cadence defined above.
