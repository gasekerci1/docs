# Non-Goals

Current status: canonical non-goals and prohibited claims list.

This document does not approve private beta, public MVP build, production readiness, public launch, implementation, or compliance claims.

## BecomingOS Must Not Claim Or Provide

- Therapy.
- Diagnosis.
- Treatment.
- Crisis intervention.
- Emergency monitoring.
- Legal advice.
- Medical advice.
- Financial advice.
- Addiction treatment advice.
- Professional advice or professional replacement.
- Guaranteed outcomes.
- Guaranteed behaviour change.
- Guaranteed wellbeing, productivity, financial, health, or clinical results.
- Public build approval.
- Public MVP approval.
- Production readiness.
- Public launch readiness.
- Unsupported market, pricing, willingness-to-pay, demand, or target segment claims.
- Legal, privacy, medical, security, regulatory, accessibility, or AI safety compliance without completed review and approval.

## Product And Data Non-Goals

- No automatic Support Circle alerts without explicit prior user consent and approved rules.
- No Support Circle crisis response or emergency rescue positioning.
- No use of rejected, deleted, unconfirmed, expired, archived, or consent-withdrawn data for AI, retrieval, recommendations, Today plans, Ask AI, analytics, embeddings, or future outputs.
- No durable memory without explicit user confirmation.
- No AI provider use with live user data without approved provider retention, logging, training-use, deletion, subprocessor, transfer, security, and contractual terms.
- No crisis or safety content as product memory.
- No sensitive free text in analytics unless separately approved by privacy/legal/safety owners.
- No production reuse of beta data without renewed consent.

## Stage Non-Goals

- Private beta planning does not approve private beta start.
- Private beta approval, if later granted, does not approve public MVP/build.
- Public MVP/build approval, if later granted, does not approve production readiness.
- Readiness reports, app/MVP audit files, and older implementation-adjacent files are evidence only unless later owner approvals say otherwise.
