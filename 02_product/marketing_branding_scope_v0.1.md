# Marketing & Branding Scope v0.1

## Status

Planning document only. This does not approve a public launch, MVP build, production readiness, paid advertising, or claims of validated demand.

BecomingOS remains pre-MVP with limited private beta planning only. All market, pricing, conversion, positioning, and audience assumptions must be treated as unvalidated until tested.

## Product Context

External product name: **BecomingOS**.

Internal repository/project alias: **lifeOS**.

Positioning category:

> A private AI life operating system for self-discovery, values, goals, decisions, daily planning, memory, and future-self alignment.

BecomingOS must not be positioned as therapy, diagnosis, crisis support, medical advice, legal advice, financial advice, addiction treatment, or a replacement for counsellors, psychologists, doctors, emergency services, legal professionals, or financial professionals.

## Recommended Core Positioning

### Primary Positioning Statement

> BecomingOS helps people understand themselves, remember what matters, plan their days, and make decisions aligned with the person they want to become.

### Short Brand Line

> Build the future version of yourself.

### Product One-Liner

> BecomingOS is a private AI life operating system that helps you organise your values, goals, memories, decisions, and daily actions around who you want to become.

### Practical User Promise

> Understand yourself. Remember what matters. Act like your future self.

## Target Audience

### Best First Audience

Overwhelmed self-improvement users who already use notes, calendars, journals, reminders, ChatGPT, habit apps, or personal development content but lack one joined-up system that remembers their values, goals, priorities, and commitments.

### Early Sub-Audiences

1. **Entrepreneurs and builders**
   - Need decision support, focus, planning, values alignment, and accountability.

2. **Carers and responsibility-heavy users**
   - Need organisation, self-awareness, reminders, boundaries, and life-priority clarity.

3. **People rebuilding routines**
   - Need structure, self-reflection, small steps, and daily follow-through.

4. **Self-help and journaling users**
   - Already want growth but need a personalised system instead of generic advice.

5. **Disorganised or overwhelmed users**
   - Need planning, reminders, memory, and simplified next actions. Do not make ADHD or medical claims unless legally and clinically reviewed.

## Core Differentiation

BecomingOS is not just another habit tracker, journal, calendar, or generic chatbot.

It combines:

1. Guided self-discovery.
2. User-confirmed AI memory.
3. Future-self profile.
4. Decision guidance using user-approved context.
5. Daily planning around priorities and commitments.
6. Life capture through text, documents, photos, voice, and video.
7. Optional support circle and accountability controls.
8. Privacy and consent as core product features.

## Website Landing Page Goal

The first landing page should not attempt to sell the complete future product.

Primary goal:

> Collect private beta/waitlist signups from the right early users and validate which message resonates.

Secondary goals:

- Explain the product clearly in under 10 seconds.
- Communicate non-clinical boundaries.
- Build trust around AI memory and privacy.
- Identify user segments with the strongest demand.
- Gather research leads for private beta interviews.

## Landing Page Required Sections

### 1. Hero

Required content:

- Headline.
- Subheadline.
- CTA.

Recommended copy:

> Build the future version of yourself.
>
> BecomingOS is a private AI life operating system that helps you understand yourself, remember what matters, plan your days, and make decisions aligned with who you want to become.
>
> Non-clinical. Private by design. You control what the AI remembers.
>

### 2. Problem

Explain the real-world problem:

- You forget your own priorities.
- Your calendar does not reflect your values.
- Your goals are scattered across notes, reminders, journals, and thoughts.
- Generic AI gives generic advice.
- You know what you want to change, but life pulls you back.

### 3. Solution

Explain BecomingOS simply:

> BecomingOS connects your values, goals, memories, decisions, tasks, and daily plan into one personal AI operating system.

Feature cards:

1. Self-Discovery Journey.
2. Memory Engine.
3. Future Self Builder.
4. Decision Engine.
5. Today Plan.
6. Life Capture.
7. Support Circle.

### 4. How It Works

Use four steps:

1. Answer guided questions.
2. Confirm what the AI should remember.
3. Build your Future Self profile.
4. Use AI to plan, decide, and stay aligned.

### 5. Product Preview

Required mockups:

- Onboarding question screen.
- Memory confirmation screen.
- Future Self profile.
- Decision guidance screen.
- Today Plan screen.
- Life Capture upload screen.

### 6. Privacy and Trust

Required messages:

- Nothing becomes memory without user confirmation.
- Users can edit or delete saved memories.
- Rejected or deleted memories are not used.
- The app is not therapy, diagnosis, crisis support, medical advice, legal advice, or financial advice.
- Private beta data requires explicit consent and retention/deletion rules.

### 7. Who It Is For

Recommended copy:

> BecomingOS is for people who are serious about becoming more organised, self-aware, and aligned, but need more than motivation.

Include:

- entrepreneurs
- carers
- busy parents
- people rebuilding routines
- people working on discipline
- people already using journaling, ChatGPT, calendars, notes, or habit apps

### 8. Who It Is Not For

Recommended copy:

> BecomingOS is not a therapist, emergency service, diagnosis tool, crisis monitor, or replacement for professional support.

### 9. Signup CTA

Recommended CTA:

> Join the Private Beta.

Recommended fields:

- first name
- email
- main reason you are interested
- what you are trying to improve right now
- optional consent checkbox for research contact

### 10. FAQ

Required FAQ topics:

- What is BecomingOS?
- Is it therapy?
- What does the AI remember?
- Can I delete my data?
- Is my data used to train AI?
- When will the beta open?
- Who is the beta for?
- Will it connect to calendar or wearables?
- Can I use photos, documents, voice, and video?
- What happens if I am in crisis?

This is directional only and requires design validation.

- Text: deep charcoal.
- Primary accent: deep green, indigo, or warm blue.
- Secondary accent: sand, clay, or soft gold.
