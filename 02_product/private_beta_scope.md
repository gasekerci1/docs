# Private Beta Scope

## Definition

Private beta means limited testing with trusted participants under documented safeguards. It is not a public MVP, public build, production release, commercial launch, or production-readiness approval.

Suggested participant limit: 5-10 trusted testers.

## Eligibility

Private beta participants must be trusted testers who receive and accept an explicit beta consent notice before providing real data.

Participants must understand that BecomingOS is educational, non-clinical, pre-MVP, and experimental.

## Real Data

Real data may be processed only with explicit consent.

Users decide what to provide. Data collection must be explained clearly and kept minimal.

## Required Safeguards

- Explicit beta consent notice.
- Separate beta environment.
- Minimal logging.
- Clear purpose for each data category collected.
- Defined retention period, with 90 days suggested.
- User ability to edit or delete provided data.
- User ability to withdraw from beta.
- Access/export process where applicable.
- AI provider disclosure before AI processing.
- Post-beta deletion or anonymisation.
- No production reuse without renewed consent.

## Boundaries

Private beta is non-public, non-commercial, and non-clinical.

BecomingOS must not provide therapy, diagnosis, treatment, crisis intervention, emergency monitoring, legal advice, medical advice, financial advice, addiction treatment advice, or other professional advice.
