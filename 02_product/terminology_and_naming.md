# Terminology And Naming

## Approved Naming

BecomingOS is the external product name.

lifeOS is an internal alias and repository alias. It is not the marketed name.

## Term Treatment

| Term | Treatment |
|---|---|
| BecomingOS | Approved external product name. Use in canonical product, privacy, safety, AI, beta, and repository-facing documentation. |
| lifeOS | Internal repository alias. Preserve for traceability where older documents use it. Do not use as the marketed name. |
| LifeOS | Prior or variant styling. Treat as historical/draft wording unless quoted from source evidence. |
| Life OS | Prior or variant styling. Treat as historical/draft wording unless quoted from source evidence. |
| My OS | Product concept term from older memory/product documents. Treat as supporting evidence, not the external product name. |
| personal alpha | Historical or implementation-adjacent stage label. Treat as evidence only; it does not approve current build or production scope. |
| prototype | May describe low-fidelity or internal validation work. It does not approve real data processing unless private beta safeguards apply. |
| MVP | Candidate planning term only. It does not mean public build approval, production readiness, or commercial launch. |
| private beta | Limited trusted participant testing with explicit consent and safeguards. It does not equal public MVP or production approval. |

## Usage Rules

- Use BecomingOS in canonical documentation.
- Use lifeOS only when explaining internal repository aliases or tracing older source material.
- Do not silently rewrite older source references; preserve traceability.
- Do not use MVP, beta, build-ready, production-ready, or launch-ready language unless the document explicitly says the status is conceptual or not approved.
