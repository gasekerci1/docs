# Landing Page Requirements v0.1

## Status

Planning document only. This does not approve public launch, MVP build, production readiness, or commercial release.

The first landing page is a validation asset for private beta/waitlist interest only.

Primary objective:

Secondary objectives:

- Explain BecomingOS clearly in under 10 seconds.
- Validate core positioning.
- Identify strongest audience segment.
- Communicate privacy and non-clinical boundaries.
- Collect research leads for private beta interviews.

## 10. FAQ Section

### What is BecomingOS?

```text
BecomingOS is a private AI life operating system for self-discovery, values, goals, memory, decisions, and daily planning.
```

### Is BecomingOS therapy?

```text
No. BecomingOS is non-clinical. It is not therapy, diagnosis, crisis support, or treatment. It is a reflection and planning system.
```

### What does the AI remember?

```text
Only user-approved memories should become durable memory. The system may suggest memory drafts, but the user must approve, edit, or reject them.
```

### Can I delete my data?

```text
The product direction requires users to be able to edit and delete saved memories and withdraw consent. Final implementation details require privacy and technical review.
```

### Is my data used to train AI?

```text
No public claim should be made until AI vendor terms and data processing rules are confirmed. The intended position is that personal beta data should not be used for training without explicit consent.
```

### When will the beta open?

```text
The private beta is not publicly scheduled yet. Selected testers may be invited after consent, privacy, safety, and research documents are ready.
```

### Who is the beta for?

```text
The beta is for trusted early testers who understand the product is pre-MVP, non-clinical, and feedback-focused.
```

### Will it connect to calendar or wearables?

```text
The product direction includes optional calendar, tasks, and health/wearable integrations with explicit consent. These features require technical and privacy review before live use.
```

### Can I use photos, documents, voice, and video?

```text
The product direction includes multimodal life capture, including photos, documents, voice notes, and videos. Users must avoid uploading third-party or sensitive information they are not allowed to share.
```

### What happens if I am in crisis?

```text
BecomingOS is not an emergency service and does not provide crisis monitoring. In an emergency, users should contact local emergency services or crisis support services.
```

## Acceptance Criteria

The landing page is ready for private-beta validation when:

- A user can understand the product in under 10 seconds.
- The page has a single clear CTA.
- Non-clinical boundaries are visible.
- Memory/privacy explanation is clear.
- Signup form captures useful research context.
- No unvalidated outcome, clinical, legal, financial, or compliance claims are made.
- All examples use fictional, non-sensitive data.
