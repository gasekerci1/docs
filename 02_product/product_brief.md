# Product Brief

## Product Identity

BecomingOS is the external product name. lifeOS is an internal repository alias and is not the marketed name.

## Vision

BecomingOS is an educational, non-clinical reflection and action-planning system that helps people understand themselves, clarify what matters, imagine a future self, make better-aligned decisions, and turn insight into daily action.

The product is intended to be calm, clear, respectful, non-shaming, and practical. It should support user agency rather than prescribe who a user is or what they must do.

## Current Stage

BecomingOS is pre-MVP with limited private beta planning.

The core specification remains conceptual. No public build, public MVP, commercial release, or production readiness is approved.

A small private beta may be planned only for trusted participants with explicit consent, a separate beta environment, minimal logging, defined retention, and post-beta deletion or anonymisation.

## Target Needs

BecomingOS is designed around these user needs:

- Understand personal values, patterns, priorities, constraints, and motivations.
- Convert reflection into concrete plans and decisions.
- Review AI-generated interpretations before anything becomes durable memory.
- Keep personal data under user control.
- Receive recommendations that explain which user-confirmed context influenced them.
- Use low-intensity planning support without clinical, emergency, or professional-advice claims.

## Product Pillars

- Self-Discovery Journey: guided reflection and structured self-understanding.
- Memory Engine: user-confirmed memory that can inform later drafts and recommendations.
- Future Self Builder: future-oriented reflection and planning.
- Decision Engine: draft options and trade-offs for user review.
- Daily Plan System: practical next actions and daily planning.
- Life Signals and Wearables Integration: conceptual use of user-approved signals.
- Life Capture / Multimodal Input: conceptual capture of user-provided context.
- Support Circle and Accountability: optional user-controlled support context.

## Boundaries

BecomingOS is not therapy, diagnosis, treatment, crisis intervention, emergency monitoring, legal advice, medical advice, financial advice, addiction treatment advice, or a replacement for qualified professional support.

AI outputs are drafts. Users must review them. Nothing becomes durable memory without explicit user confirmation.
