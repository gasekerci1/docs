# Social Content Launch Pack v0.1

## 1. Status and Claim Boundaries

This document is copy planning only for the BecomingOS private beta/waitlist phase.

It does not approve:

- public launch
- paid advertising
- MVP build
- production readiness
- clinical, crisis, medical, legal, financial, or guaranteed outcome claims
- positioning BecomingOS as therapy or a replacement for professional support

BecomingOS remains pre-MVP with limited private beta planning only. All social content must be treated as validation copy until tested with real users.

## 2. Content Pillars

| Pillar | Purpose | Safe Message Direction | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Future self | Communicate the core aspiration | Build toward the person you want to become | Guaranteed transformation |
| Self-awareness | Explain guided reflection | Understand values, priorities, patterns, and commitments | Diagnosis, treatment, or clinical language |
| AI memory | Explain user-confirmed memory | The AI only remembers what the user approves | Claims about vendor training or security unless reviewed |
| Daily planning | Show practical use | Turn priorities into a realistic daily plan | Productivity guarantees |
| Decision-making | Show personalised guidance | Compare choices against user-approved context | Legal, medical, financial, or crisis advice |
| Founder journey | Build trust through the build-in-public story | Explain why the product exists and what is being tested | Overclaiming progress or readiness |
| Privacy and trust | Reduce AI-memory concern | User control, edit/delete direction, non-clinical boundary | Compliance claims unless reviewed |

## 13. Claims to Avoid

Do not publish claims that say or imply:

- BecomingOS is therapy, diagnosis, treatment, crisis support, or clinical support.
- BecomingOS replaces psychologists, counsellors, doctors, emergency services, lawyers, or financial advisers.
- BecomingOS cures, fixes, heals, treats, or guarantees transformation.
- BecomingOS improves productivity, mental health, income, relationships, addiction outcomes, or wellbeing by a measured percentage.
- Market demand, pricing, retention, conversion, or willingness to pay has been validated unless evidence exists.
- User data is never used for AI training unless vendor terms and privacy review support that claim.
- BecomingOS is fully secure, compliant, production-ready, or public-launch ready.
- The beta is open to everyone or ready for commercial use.
- The app monitors users in real time or automatically intervenes in crisis situations.
