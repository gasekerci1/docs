# MVP Scope

Current status: candidate planning only.

This document does not approve public MVP build, implementation, production readiness, public launch, commercial launch, or compliance claims.

Product source of truth: `docs/02_product/becomingos_product_specification.docx`.

## Source-Of-Truth Product Vision

BecomingOS is an educational, non-clinical reflection and action-planning system. It helps users explore values, patterns, priorities, future-self direction, decisions, and practical daily action.

BecomingOS is not therapy, diagnosis, treatment, crisis intervention, emergency monitoring, legal advice, medical advice, financial advice, addiction treatment advice, or a replacement for professional support.

## Private Beta Learning Scope

Private beta is separate from public MVP/build. It may be planned only for trusted participants with explicit consent, separate beta environment, minimal logging, defined retention, deletion or anonymisation after beta, and no production reuse without renewed consent.

Private beta learning may explore:

- Consent and product-boundary comprehension.
- AI draft and memory confirmation clarity.
- Today Directive usefulness.
- Bad Day Mode usefulness.
- Support Circle comfort and permission comprehension.
- Privacy comfort with optional data categories.
- Safety boundary comprehension.

Private beta findings, if beta runs, are research evidence only. They do not automatically approve public MVP/build.

## Candidate Public MVP Scope

Candidate pillars for future public MVP consideration:

- Self-Discovery Journey.
- Memory Engine with explicit user confirmation.
- Future Self Builder.
- Decision Engine with AI drafts and user review.
- Daily Plan System and Today Directive.
- Evidence-Based Growth Loop: diagnosis flow, if-then plan, completion tracking, failure diagnosis, adaptive reset.
- Bad Day Mode.
- Optional Support Circle permissions.
- Privacy-safe analytics, if approved.

These pillars are unapproved for public build until gates in `00_repository_control/public_mvp_build_gate_register.md` pass.

## Excluded Or Post-MVP Scope

Excluded from current MVP approval:

- Public build or launch.
- Production readiness.
- Production reuse of beta data without renewed consent.
- Therapy, diagnosis, treatment, crisis intervention, or emergency monitoring.
- Legal, medical, financial, addiction treatment, or other professional advice.
- Automatic Support Circle alerts without explicit prior consent and approved rules.
- Use of rejected, deleted, unconfirmed, expired, archived, or consent-withdrawn data.
- AI provider processing without approved provider terms.
- Wearables, documents, photos, audio, video, or support contacts in public MVP without specific consent, privacy, safety, security, and AI/data approval.
- Market, pricing, demand, or outcome claims without validation.

## Approval Boundary

No public MVP/build is approved. This document defines candidate planning scope only.
