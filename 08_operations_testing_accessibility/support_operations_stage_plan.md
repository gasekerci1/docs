# Support Operations Stage Plan

Current status: support operations planning only.

This document does not approve private beta, public MVP build, production readiness, public launch, operational readiness, or compliance claims.

## Support Boundary

Support means product, account, access, data-rights, and help support. It is not therapy, crisis rescue, emergency monitoring, diagnosis, treatment, legal advice, medical advice, financial advice, addiction treatment advice, or professional support.

If immediate danger or crisis appears, support materials may signpost appropriate emergency or human support, but BecomingOS must not imply live rescue or monitoring.

## Stage Model

| Stage | Support posture | Staffing assumption | Current status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Documentation planning | No live user support; documentation review only. | Not applicable unless owner assigns documentation contacts. | Active planning |
| Private beta | Limited trusted tester support only if beta is approved. Must cover consent, withdrawal, deletion/export/access, safety boundary questions, and issue reporting. | Unknown until owner assigns named beta support/contact owner. | NO-GO |
| Public MVP | Public-facing support model required before build approval, including response channels, escalation, data rights, incident handoff, and support copy review. | Unknown until operations/support owner approves. | NO-GO |
| Production | Production support operations required before launch, including staffing, coverage, escalation, provider incidents, data rights, accessibility support, analytics/privacy issues, and incident response. | Unknown until production operations plan is approved. | NO-GO |

## Required Support Capabilities Before Production

- Approved support scope and exclusions.
- Named operations/support owner.
- Approved support channels.
- Approved response-time targets, if any.
- Privacy/legal review of support copy and data handling.
- Safety review of crisis/signposting boundaries.
- Data deletion/export/access support process.
- Incident response handoff.
- Provider incident handoff.
- Accessibility support pathway.
- Analytics/privacy issue pathway.
- Support training and prohibited-claims guidance.

## Staffing Assumptions

Support staffing assumptions are unknown unless approved by operations/support owner. Do not assume 24/7 support, crisis monitoring, emergency response, clinical support, legal support, medical support, financial support, or production coverage.

## Stage Rule

Support readiness must be approved separately for each stage. Private beta support readiness does not approve public MVP support readiness, and public MVP support readiness does not approve production support readiness.
