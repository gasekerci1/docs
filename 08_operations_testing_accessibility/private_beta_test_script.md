# Private Beta Test Script

Current status: approval-ready draft. This script does not approve private beta, implementation, public MVP build, production readiness, or compliance claims.

Use only after consent, privacy/legal/safety/security/AI provider reviews, and owner go/no-go approval are complete.

## Pre-Test Briefing

Thank you for helping test BecomingOS. This is a limited, non-public private beta. BecomingOS is pre-MVP, experimental, educational, and non-clinical.

It is not therapy, diagnosis, treatment, crisis support, emergency monitoring, legal advice, medical advice, financial advice, addiction treatment advice, or a replacement for professional help.

You can pause, skip any task, decline optional data categories, or withdraw.

## Consent Confirmation

Ask:

1. Have you read the private beta consent notice?
2. Do you understand the data categories you may choose to provide?
3. Do you understand AI outputs are drafts that you review?
4. Do you understand nothing becomes durable memory unless you confirm it?
5. Do you understand you can request access/export, correction, deletion, or withdrawal?

Do not continue if consent is unclear.

## Onboarding Tasks

1. Review the onboarding explanation.
2. Identify what BecomingOS is for in your own words.
3. Identify what BecomingOS is not for in your own words.
4. Choose one planning or reflection area you are comfortable testing.

Comprehension questions:

- What parts felt clear?
- What parts felt uncertain?
- Did anything make the product sound clinical or emergency-related?

## Memory Confirmation Tasks

1. Review an AI-drafted memory candidate.
2. Choose edit, reject, or confirm.
3. Explain what you think happens after each choice.

Questions:

- Was it clear that the AI output was a draft?
- Was it clear what becomes durable memory?
- Was it clear how to reject or delete something?

## Today Directive Tasks

1. Review a Today Directive.
2. Check whether the rationale is understandable.
3. Edit or reject the directive if it does not fit.

Questions:

- Did the directive feel practical?
- Did the rationale feel grounded in your confirmed context?
- Did you feel in control of the final action?

## Bad Day Mode Tasks

1. Review a full action and a reduced version.
2. Choose whether the reduced version would help on a low-capacity day.

Questions:

- Did Bad Day Mode feel supportive?
- Did any wording feel shaming or pressuring?
- Was it clear this is planning support, not therapy or crisis support?

## Life Capture Task

1. Review optional input categories such as manual entry, document, photo, audio, or video.
2. Choose one category you would be comfortable testing, or decline.

Questions:

- Was optionality clear?
- Was the purpose clear?
- Did you know how to avoid a data category?

## Support Circle Comfort Questions

- Would you want to use Support Circle in beta?
- What would you be comfortable sharing?
- What should never be shared?
- Was it clear there is no automatic alerting without explicit prior consent?
- Was it clear Support Circle is not crisis response?

## Privacy Comfort Questions

- Which data categories felt acceptable?
- Which felt too sensitive?
- Was retention clear?
- Was deletion or withdrawal clear?
- Was AI provider disclosure clear enough?

## Safety Boundary Comprehension Questions

- What would you do if you were in immediate danger?
- Did anything imply BecomingOS monitors you in real time?
- Did anything imply therapy, diagnosis, treatment, or professional advice?
- Did crisis signposting feel clear without feeling like emergency monitoring?

## Post-Test Debrief

Ask:

- What was most useful?
- What was confusing?
- What felt too sensitive?
- What would need to change before you would trust the beta?
- Do you want any data corrected, exported, deleted, or withdrawn?

Record findings without unnecessary sensitive free text.
