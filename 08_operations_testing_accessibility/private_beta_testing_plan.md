# Private Beta Testing Plan

Current status: planning document only. This document does not approve private beta start, public MVP build, production readiness, implementation work, or compliance claims.

## Participant Scope

Suggested private beta size: 5-10 trusted testers.

Participants must receive and accept the beta consent notice before providing real data.

## Test Goals

- Usability: Can participants complete the beta flows without confusion?
- Comprehension: Do participants understand BecomingOS is pre-MVP, experimental, educational, and non-clinical?
- Trust: Do participants understand AI outputs are drafts?
- Consent clarity: Do participants understand data categories, optionality, retention, deletion, export/access, withdrawal, AI provider disclosure, and no production reuse?
- AI memory confirmation clarity: Do participants understand what becomes durable memory and what does not?
- Today Directive usefulness: Do participants understand and find the daily action useful?
- Bad Day Mode usefulness: Does the reduced plan feel supportive and non-shaming?
- Support Circle comfort: Do participants understand permissions and feel control over sharing?
- Privacy comfort: Do participants feel comfortable with data category choices and controls?
- Safety boundary comprehension: Do participants understand BecomingOS is not emergency service, therapy, diagnosis, treatment, or professional advice?

## Pre-Test Steps

- Confirm final beta scope.
- Confirm participant criteria and invite list.
- Complete privacy/legal/safety/security/AI provider reviews.
- Confirm separate beta environment requirements.
- Confirm retention/deletion workflow.
- Confirm consent notice.
- Confirm stop criteria and support contact.
- Confirm owner go/no-go decision.

## During-Test Steps

- Track consent acceptance and data category choices.
- Observe usability and comprehension issues.
- Capture participant feedback without storing unnecessary sensitive free text.
- Monitor stop criteria.
- Record only approved privacy-safe analytics.
- Review any safety events under the safety logging policy.

## Post-Test Steps

- Summarise findings without overstating validation.
- Separate evidence, assumptions, and open issues.
- Document participant withdrawal/deletion requests.
- Delete or anonymise beta data according to policy.
- Decide whether renewed consent is needed for any future use.
- Update blocker register and evidence register.

## Stop Criteria

Pause or stop beta if:

- Consent wording is misunderstood in a material way.
- Participants believe the product provides therapy, diagnosis, treatment, crisis response, or professional advice.
- AI outputs appear to be treated as authoritative decisions.
- Deleted/rejected/unconfirmed data influences AI or analytics.
- Safety/crisis content is captured as product memory.
- Sensitive data appears in analytics unexpectedly.
- AI provider terms are unresolved or violated.
- Environment separation or access controls fail.
- Any owner, safety, privacy/legal, security, or research lead calls a stop.
