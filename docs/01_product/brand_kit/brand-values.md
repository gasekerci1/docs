# LifeOS Brand Values

## Core brand values

- **Clarity** — help users see what matters and what to do next.
- **Agency** — users confirm, edit, reject, save, or delete their own information.
- **Structure** — turn scattered goals, patterns, and reflections into a usable operating system.
- **Warmth** — feel human and calm, not cold or clinical.
- **Practicality** — favour daily action over motivational hype.
- **Safety** — never position LifeOS as therapy, diagnosis, treatment, crisis support, legal advice, or financial advice.
- **Privacy** — memory and personal insight must be user-owned and user-confirmed.
- **Growth** — support reflection, review, and adjustment without pretending to define the user.

## Brand personality

LifeOS should feel like a calm strategist and private command centre.

It should not feel like a therapist, guru, productivity bro, social app, or clinical assessment tool.

## Recommended direction

Use the OS Stack / layered system mark as the primary visual identity. Keep the warm premium palette, rounded UI, calm copy, and explicit AI-confirmation language across the app.
