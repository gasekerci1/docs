# Private Beta Test Script

Current status: limited trusted private beta script only. This script does not approve public MVP/build, production readiness, public launch, compliance claims, broad AI use, multimodal uploads, wearables/passive monitoring, vector stores, or crisis response.

## Pre-Test Introduction

Thank the participant for joining the limited private beta. Explain that BecomingOS is pre-MVP, experimental, educational, and non-clinical. Confirm the session is for feedback on clarity, trust, usefulness, boundaries, and operational safety.

## Consent Confirmation

- [ ] Confirm participant received the approved consent notice.
- [ ] Confirm participant acknowledged consent.
- [ ] Confirm participant understands participation is optional.
- [ ] Confirm participant understands they may request access/export, correction, deletion, or withdrawal.
- [ ] Confirm participant understands beta data will not be reused in production without renewed consent.

## Non-Clinical Boundary Reminder

Remind the participant that BecomingOS is not therapy, diagnosis, treatment, crisis support, emergency monitoring, medical advice, legal advice, financial advice, addiction treatment advice, or a replacement for professional help. For emergencies, participants should contact local emergency services or appropriate crisis support.

## Onboarding Test

- Ask participant to complete the approved text-only onboarding/reflection flow.
- Observe confusion, friction, skipped sections, and unclear wording.
- Do not request sensitive free text, crisis details, medical details, legal details, financial details, or uploaded files.

## AI Text Response Test

- Use only approved text-only prompts.
- Confirm no files, images, audio, video, wearable data, passive-monitoring data, or unapproved vector retrieval are used.
- Ask participant whether the AI response feels clear, bounded, useful, and easy to reject or edit.
- Remind participant AI outputs are drafts requiring review.

## Memory Confirmation Test If In Scope

- Show draft memory candidate.
- Ask participant to accept, edit, reject, or skip.
- Confirm nothing becomes durable memory without explicit confirmation.
- Confirm rejected, deleted, expired, withdrawn, or unconfirmed data is excluded from memory, retrieval, analytics, embeddings, and future outputs.

## Feedback Questions

- What was clear?
- What was confusing?
- What felt useful?
- What felt unsafe or too personal?
- Did any wording imply therapy, diagnosis, treatment, crisis response, or professional advice?
- Did the AI response feel appropriately limited?
- Did the consent and data controls feel understandable?
- What should be removed before further testing?

## Withdrawal/Deletion Reminder

Remind the participant that they may request access/export, correction, deletion, withdrawal from beta, or withdrawal of consent for future processing through the approved support channel.

## Operator Notes

- Record participant ID only.
- Record minimal operational observations.
- Do not record sensitive free text, crisis text, private reflections, medical details, legal details, financial details, or uploaded content.
- Escalate any stop-criteria trigger immediately.
