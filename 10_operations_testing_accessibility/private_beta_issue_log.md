# Private Beta Issue Log

Current status: limited trusted private beta issue log template only. Record minimal operational information. Do not store sensitive free text, crisis text, private reflections, medical details, legal details, financial details, or uploaded content.

## Severity Definitions

- Low: Minor usability, wording, or documentation issue with no immediate privacy, safety, consent, security, or AI-scope risk.
- Medium: Issue that affects comprehension, trust, consent clarity, workflow completion, or tester experience but does not trigger stop criteria.
- High: Issue that may affect privacy, safety, consent, deletion/export/withdrawal, AI boundaries, non-clinical boundaries, or tester distress. Owner review required.
- Critical: Stop-criteria issue, serious privacy/security/safety concern, consent failure, unapproved data flow, unsafe AI output, crisis misuse, or participant distress requiring immediate pause/escalation.

## Handling Rules

- Use participant IDs only.
- Keep notes minimal and administrative.
- If a critical issue occurs, pause affected testing and complete the stop criteria checklist.
- Do not use issue log content for analytics, memory, embeddings, training, or future AI outputs.
