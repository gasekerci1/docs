## Questions

1. How clear was the onboarding flow?
2. Which parts were confusing?
3. How much did you trust the experience and why?
4. What felt useful?
5. What felt unnecessary or should be removed?
6. Did anything feel unsafe, too personal, or emotionally uncomfortable?
7. Did you have any privacy concerns?
8. Did the consent notice and data rights feel understandable?
9. Did the AI text response feel clear, bounded, and easy to review?
10. Did the AI response ever feel too authoritative, clinical, therapeutic, legal, medical, financial, or crisis-oriented?
11. Was it clear that AI outputs are drafts and not professional advice?
12. Was it clear how to reject, edit, or confirm a memory candidate?
13. Was it clear how to request deletion, export/access, correction, or withdrawal?
14. What onboarding friction did you notice?
15. What felt most valuable?
16. What should not be included in the private beta?
17. Would you be willing to continue testing within the same limited private beta boundaries?
18. Is there anything else the beta operators should know, excluding sensitive free text or crisis details?

## Market/Pricing Note

Any statements about willingness to continue, willingness to pay, pricing, conversion, growth, or market demand are assumptions only until validated through a separately approved research process.

## Operator Handling

- Summarise themes without storing sensitive free text.
- Do not store crisis text, medical/therapy content, relationship details, addiction details, uploaded document text, or personal secrets in analytics.
- Escalate safety, privacy, consent, security, or AI-scope concerns through the issue log.
